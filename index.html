02

Approach

Most of what I build sits where web development meets security. I care about the parts people skip: the error case, the slow connection, the keyboard user.

Break it first

I learn a system by attacking it. Reading how SQL injection and XSS actually land taught me more about web frameworks than any tutorial, and it is why my own apps validate at the boundary instead of trusting the client.

Ship without a build step

TaskFlow and this site are plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No framework, no bundler, nothing to go stale. They load instantly on a bad connection, which is the point.

Finish the unglamorous part

A scanner that finds a bug and cannot explain it is half a tool. Mine writes exploitation steps and a readable report, because the report is what someone actually acts on.
